1. The Target Conference: IJCNN
While WCCI is the "umbrella" event, TinyML falls squarely into the Neural Networks domain. Manuel Roveri and Massimo Pavan have a history of organizing this specific session within the IJCNN track of WCCI.

2. How to Submit
When you log into the WCCI 2026 Submission Portal (typically using the Linklings system), follow these steps:

Step 1: Choose the conference IJCNN 2026.

Step 2: Look for a field often labeled "Main Research Topic" or "Paper Track."

Step 3: Scroll past the regular technical tracks to the "Special Sessions" list. You are looking for something like:

"IJCNN SS: Special Session on Tiny Machine Learning (TinyML)"
